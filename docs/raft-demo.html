Things to try

  • Crash the leader. The followers' timers run out, one becomes a candidate and wins. Writes are refused until the new leader is in place.
  • Crash two followers, then write. Entries still commit, because the leader plus two others is 3 of 5.
  • Crash a third follower. New entries stay amber (not committed), because no majority can store them.
  • Restart a server that missed entries. Watch the hollow "rejected" replies: the leader backs up one entry at a time until the logs match, then fills in the gap.
